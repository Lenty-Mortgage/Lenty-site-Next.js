Why this matters

Deals don't fall through on the buyer

Most deals don't fall through because the buyer changed their mind. They fall through because the wrong broker, the wrong bank or a late document stalled the financing. The agent loses the commission, the developer loses the unit, the buyer loses the home.

Four cases we take

  • Non-resident buyers
  • Self-employed and business owners
  • Documents that arrive late
  • The wrong bank that damages the credit profile

How we work

  • Structured before submission. The file goes in once, the way the bank reads it.
  • The right bank, not the nearest one.
  • One advisor on the whole case, never passed between desks.
  • Honest timelines: salaried with full documents 24–48 h.

Partner questions

Will you contact my client about other properties?

No. We don't sell property and never will.

Is the partnership exclusive?

No. Send us the cases where you want a specialist.

How is the commission calculated and when is it paid?

0.3% of the loan, 45 days after disbursal, with a date you can see.

Which cases do you take?

All mortgages in the UAE, and especially the complex ones: non-residents, self-employed and business owners, declined elsewhere, off-plan handover, non-standard income.

How do I see the status of a case?

In the Lenty Partners App, or by WhatsApp from your advisor.
