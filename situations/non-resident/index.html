Living outside the UAE

Buy in Dubai while you live abroad

You don't need UAE residency for a Dubai mortgage. You need the bank whose rules fit your country, your currency and your income. That's the part we handle.

How banks see this case

  • ✓What they acceptSalaried or company income in EUR, GBP, USD and other major currencies; 6 months of statements with a healthy average balance.
  • ✗What they don'tIncome without statements, cash-only businesses, a few restricted nationalities at some banks.
  • ◎What decides itWhich bank, your currency, and whether the property is ready or off-plan (off-plan is capped at 50%).

How much cash you'll need

Non-residents usually borrow up to 50–60% of the price. On top of the down payment come the fees banks don't finance.

Example, AED 2,000,000 ready apartment, 50% financing: down payment 1,000,000 + DLD 80,580 + agent 42,000 + registration, valuation and bank fees ≈ 20,500. Total ≈ AED 1.14M.

Documents and timeline

Salaried

  • Passport (all pages)
  • 6 months of payslips and a salary certificate
  • 6 months of personal bank statements
  • Proof of address
  • Credit report from your country (some banks)

Business owner

  • Passport
  • Certificate of incorporation, shareholder register, articles
  • 6–12 months of company and personal statements
  • Audited accounts, usually 2 years
  • Proof of address

Nothing is needed for the first call.

Plan for 24–48 hours to pre-approval and 2–3 weeks from valuation to transfer. Most of the process is remote.

Questions

Do I have to travel to Dubai?

Not for the pre-approval: it runs remotely. For the transfer at the trustee office, your advisor confirms what your case needs.

Is there a fee for non-residents?

Yes. Non-resident files take more work, so we charge a fee agreed in writing before we start: AED 10,000.

Which nationalities are restricted?

It depends on the bank. A few nationalities are excluded by some banks and accepted by others. Tell us yours and we'll check.

Will the bank accept my foreign credit history?

Some banks ask for a credit report from your country; a clean UAE record isn't required because you don't have one yet.

Can I rent the property out?

Yes, with most banks. Tell us upfront so we choose a bank that allows it.
