Income isn't a simple salary

Bonus, commission, rent or a new job? Each has a bank that counts it

Banks like a fixed salary on a UAE payslip. Real income rarely looks like that. The job is to find the bank whose policy matches your income — and to show it the way they read it.

How banks see this case

  • ✓What they acceptBonus and commission shown over 12–24 months, rental income with tenancy contracts, foreign-currency salary from a recognised employer.
  • ✗What they don'tCash income, undeclared rent, income without a paper trail.
  • ◎What decides itAverages and consistency, not the best month.

How much cash you'll need

Banks finance part of the price. They don't finance the DLD fee, the agent, the trustee or the valuation. The calculator shows the number for your status and price.

Documents and timeline

Salaried

  • Passport, Emirates ID and visa
  • 6 months of payslips and a salary certificate
  • 6 months of personal bank statements
  • Proof of address

Business owner

  • Passport, Emirates ID and visa
  • Trade licence and company documents
  • 6–12 months of company and personal statements
  • Audited accounts, usually 2 years

Nothing is needed for the first call.

Plan for 24–48 hours to pre-approval and 2–3 weeks from valuation to transfer.

Questions

My salary is in EUR — will the bank accept it?

Foreign-currency salary from a recognised employer is accepted by several banks. Which bank depends on your currency and whether you are a resident.

I just changed jobs — do I have to wait?

Not always. Several banks accept 3 months in the new job, and a few one salary credit with a confirmation letter, especially if you worked in the UAE before. Others want 6 months. Keep your confirmation letter and your previous employer's experience letter ready.

Does rental income count?

Yes, with tenancy contracts — banks count 70–80% of it.

I'm a freelancer with several clients — what do banks want?

Banks treat freelancers like the self-employed: a licence or freelance permit and usually 6–12 months of personal bank statements with regular credits. Some programmes lend on the average monthly credits, typically up to 60–65% of the price.

Can two incomes be combined?

Yes. Spouses often apply together, and some banks accept a parent, child or sibling as co-borrower. Each bank sets a minimum per person, often AED 10–15k for the main applicant, and some limit the application to two people.
